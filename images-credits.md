its store, its staff, or its actual work. None of the live site's photos were downloaded, copied or
hotlinked.

[Pexels Licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free for commercial use, no attribution
required, and images may be modified. Attribution is recorded here anyway so every file is
traceable, and so swapping a photo later is straightforward. No watermarked, paid-only or
model-restricted image is used.

## Checking licences before swapping

If a real photo is supplied by the owner, credit is not required — it is their own work. If another
stock photo is added, record the photographer, the photo page URL and the licence in this table,
and confirm the licence allows commercial use on a business website.
